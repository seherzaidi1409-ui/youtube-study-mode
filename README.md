# YouTube Study Mode

**A Chrome extension prototype designed to reduce distracting YouTube content during study sessions.**

## Problem

Students often use YouTube to learn through tutorials, lectures, and educational videos. However, Shorts and recommendation-heavy sections can sometimes pull them away from their original study goal.

Interviews with three students suggested that this experience varies: two described YouTube-specific distractions, while one mainly used YouTube for studying without considering it a major distraction.

## Target User

Students who rely on YouTube for educational content but sometimes get distracted by Shorts or other content recommendations.

## Solution Hypothesis

If students can temporarily hide certain distracting sections of YouTube while keeping access to educational videos, they may find it easier to stay aligned with their study intentions.

This is a hypothesis that needs further testing.

## MVP Features

- A popup to switch Study Mode ON or OFF.
- CSS-based hiding of selected Shorts-related and recommendation-heavy sections.
- A simple interface showing the current mode.
- A way to return to the normal YouTube experience by switching Study Mode off.

## Core User Loop

1. Open YouTube for a study session.
2. Turn Study Mode on using the extension popup.
3. Access educational videos with selected distracting sections hidden.
4. Turn Study Mode off when normal browsing is preferred.

## Current Status

The MVP is a basic working prototype tested locally in Chrome. Its behaviour may vary across YouTube pages and layouts. It has not yet been shown to improve focus or study outcomes.

## Key Learnings

- YouTube can be both a useful learning resource and a source of distraction.
- The problem is not identical for every student.
- A focused, reversible intervention may be more appropriate than blocking YouTube entirely.
- Further testing is needed to assess usability and whether the prototype helps students maintain their study intentions.
